Your way, from the first task.

Seven steps, on this machine. Your archive stays private.

“Tick what Varyz may read. Nothing else is touched.”

What setup does

  1. 01
    Archive
    Creates your private archive at …, or keeps the one already there.
  2. 02
    Daemon
    Starts the small local service your agents ask, on 127.0.0.1 only.
  3. 03
    Agents
    Connects the agents you tick, so each task they get arrives briefed.
    • 04
      Data sources
      Indexes what you tick, on this machine. Read locally, never uploaded.
        The first indexing downloads the local models it uses: about 1.3 GB, once. Any download can be paused and carried on later.
      • 05
        Graph
        Builds the graph of what your archive says, and the personal model your agents look things up in.
      • 06
        Autosync
        Keeps all of it current in the background, from now on and at every login.
      • 07
        Classifier
        Readies the local model that sorts your agents’ questions, installing Ollama if it is missing.
        Downloads the model once: a few GB. It runs on this machine.
      Safe to run again: every step keeps what is already there. Optional sharing is off. You’ll choose before setup starts.
